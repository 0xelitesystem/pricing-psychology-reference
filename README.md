# Pricing Psychology Reference

People do not judge a price against some absolute sense of value. They judge it against other prices, against what they expected, and against how the offer is framed. Pricing psychology is the study of those reference points: anchoring, tiers, the look of the number itself, and the context around it. Used well, it presents a fair price clearly; used badly, it manipulates. This reference covers the mechanics and where the honest line sits.

## A note

This is general marketing education. It describes how people respond to prices, not how to set yours, which depends on your costs, market, and goals.
